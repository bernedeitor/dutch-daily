# iPhone acceptance check

1. Open the HTTPS app in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. Open the installed app online and wait for **Ready offline**.
2. Confirm **500 common words → Flashcards** starts with 20 learning cards. To continue earlier progress, use **Settings → Import progress** with a private backup. Reveal a meaning, rate it, close the app, and reopen. Progress should remain saved.
3. Switch to **Listening + spelling**. Dutch audio should play from the mode tap. Replay and Slow should work. Type an answer, check it, and rate it. Flashcards progress should be unaffected.
4. Select an installed **nl-NL** voice in Settings. Test “Goedemorgen”. Enable airplane mode, fully close and reopen the app, then test replay, study, switching decks/modes, and saving a rating. App and speech should work with the installed voice. If speech fails, install a Dutch voice while online and repeat.
5. Export a full backup to Files. Import it and check the preview. Add a two-column CSV, rate a card, and verify its separate progress. Reset the current mode, then Undo in Settings.
6. At your normal text size (and a larger accessibility size), check that buttons are reachable, text stays inside the card, and the spelling keyboard doesn't cover the input/check button. The app scrolls naturally when needed.

Safari and the Home Screen app may have separate progress. Transfer a backup into the installed app if you practiced in Safari first.
